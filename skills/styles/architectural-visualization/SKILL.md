# Architectural Visualization
Prioritize accurate geometry, believable scale, vertical/perspective discipline, material behavior, natural light and spatial continuity.
For interiors, keep furniture grounded and pathways plausible.
For exteriors, protect facade massing and window rhythm.
Distinguish a photographic visualization from a stylized 3D render when the medium matters.
