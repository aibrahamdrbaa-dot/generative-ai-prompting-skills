# Photorealism
Use observable photographic mechanisms: plausible perspective, natural material response, coherent light, contact shadows, exposure, depth of field and realistic environmental scale.
Avoid "hyperrealistic" as a substitute for describing what should look real.
For property images, preserve architecture before enhancing atmosphere.
For people, prioritize natural skin texture and believable anatomy without over-smoothing.
