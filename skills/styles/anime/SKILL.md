# Anime
Specify character design, line weight, eye/face conventions, shading model, color blocking, background treatment and camera composition.
Choose the boundary: clean cel animation, painterly anime background, detailed key visual, or manga-like graphic.
Avoid conflating anime with generic cartoon/chibi styling.
