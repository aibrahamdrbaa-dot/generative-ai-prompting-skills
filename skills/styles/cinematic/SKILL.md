# Cinematic
Define cinematic intent through composition, motivated lighting, color relationships, depth layers and camera movement.
For video, specify shot progression and continuity.
Use motivated practical sources and readable contrast instead of a generic "cinematic" adjective.
Avoid overusing lens numbers unless the visual effect requires them.
