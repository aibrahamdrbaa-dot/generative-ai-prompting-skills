# Product Photography
Protect silhouette, proportions, surface material, label placement and contact geometry.
Control key light, fill, edge/rim light, reflections, shadow grounding and background separation.
Never invent brand copy.
Choose hero/product-context framing intentionally: centered packshot, three-quarter hero, macro detail or environmental product shot.
