# Big Five Analysis

## Dimensions

- Openness to Experience
- Conscientiousness
- Extraversion
- Agreeableness
- Neuroticism or emotional-stability framing, depending on instrument

## Workflow

1. Identify instrument/version and score type: raw, standardized, percentile, or normed.
2. Preserve continuous scores.
3. Interpret dimensions independently before examining interactions.
4. Use behavioral examples as evidence, not proof.
5. State uncertainty from norms, population, age, culture, language, or instrument differences.

## Important

Do not collapse the five dimensions into a single personality score.

Prefer direct dimensional interpretation over forcing MBTI-style categories.

## Test handling

Use Assessment Adapter for administration and preserve the exact instrument metadata.
