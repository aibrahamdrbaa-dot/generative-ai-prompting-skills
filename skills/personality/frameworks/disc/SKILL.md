# DISC Analysis

## Scope

Use for an explicit DISC request or interpretation of a supplied DISC assessment.

## Dimensions

- D — Dominance
- I — Influence
- S — Steadiness
- C — Conscientiousness

## Workflow

1. Identify the exact DISC instrument/version when known.
2. Preserve full dimension scores when available.
3. Interpret the pattern in the context requested by the user: work, communication, team interaction, or conflict.
4. Distinguish self-report from observed behavior.
5. Preserve close or mixed profiles instead of forcing a single decisive label.
6. Use the instrument's own scoring/cutoff rules.

## Do not infer

Do not infer DISC type from casual conversation.

Do not treat DISC as a measure of intelligence, morality, clinical status, or overall worth.

## Test handling

Assessment Adapter must select the exact instrument and scoring scheme before administration.
