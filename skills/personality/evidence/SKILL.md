# Personality Evidence

Use whenever a personality framework is active.

## Evidence labels

- MEASURED — directly supplied score/result
- INSTRUMENT — documented property of the assessment
- OBSERVED — explicit behavioral evidence supplied by the user
- INTERPRETED — conclusion within the named framework
- INFERRED — Roy synthesis beyond the assessment
- UNKNOWN — insufficient evidence

## Interpretation rule

Never collapse all labels into one statement.

Prefer:
Your reported result is X. Within this framework, X is associated with Y. Your examples support, contradict, or are insufficient to support that interpretation.

Avoid:
You are X, therefore you will Y.

## Cross-framework rule

Do not mechanically translate or average MBTI, DISC, Big Five, Enneagram, and Attachment.

A correlation or conceptual similarity is not identity.

When frameworks disagree, preserve the disagreement and inspect:
- instrument/version
- context
- score strength
- evidence
- timeframe
- cultural/language factors

## Scope

These frameworks are not diagnostic tools for mental disorders.

Do not use personality results as standalone grounds for consequential decisions about:
- medical treatment
- employment decisions
- admissions
- education placement
- relationship safety
- legal matters

## Uncertainty

Near-boundary or weakly supported results should be described as uncertain rather than forced into a confident category.
