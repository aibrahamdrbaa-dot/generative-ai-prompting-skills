# DISC Analysis

## Trigger

Only on explicit DISC/personality-analysis request or interpretation of supplied DISC results.

## Core dimensions

- D: Dominance
- I: Influence
- S: Steadiness
- C: Conscientiousness

## Workflow

1. Identify the specific DISC instrument/version when known.
2. Preserve all available dimension scores; do not reduce the profile to one letter if the instrument provides a full pattern.
3. Interpret the pattern in context: work, team, communication, conflict, or another user-specified setting.
4. Distinguish self-report result from observed behavior.
5. Describe tendencies rather than fixed identity.
6. When a primary and secondary pattern are close, preserve the ambiguity rather than inventing a decisive label.

## Evidence discipline

Different DISC instruments use different item banks, scoring, norms, and reporting conventions. Never transfer cutoffs or blend rules from one instrument to another without evidence.

Treat DISC as a behavioral framework. Do not turn a DISC label into a claim about clinical status, intelligence, morality, or overall worth.

## Test handling

If assessment is requested, retrieve the exact instrument and scoring instructions used. Do not assume that all "DISC tests" are psychometrically equivalent.
