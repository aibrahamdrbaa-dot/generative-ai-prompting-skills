# Real Estate Visuals
Separate property truth from styling and atmosphere.
Preserve, when applicable: room geometry, windows/doors, ceiling, floor boundaries, fixtures, facade massing and camera perspective.
Virtual staging changes furniture/decor, not the shell.
Renovation previews identify exactly what changes.
Avoid floating furniture, extra windows, duplicated objects, impossible proportions and accidental perspective changes.
Generated/staged visuals should not be presented as untouched documentary photography when disclosure matters.
