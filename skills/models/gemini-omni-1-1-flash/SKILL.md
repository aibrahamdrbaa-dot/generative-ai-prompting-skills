# Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash
Model: `gemini-omni-1.1-flash`.
## Prompt anatomy
Shot/framing → camera motion → action → location → visual feel → lighting → timing/audio/text → continuity.
## Single-scene
When one uninterrupted scene is required, explicitly say single continuous shot / one unbroken scene / no scene cuts.
## Editing
Keep edits concise. For a local edit, state the target change and protect the rest.
## Timing
Use natural language or time ranges such as [0-3s].
## Audio
Describe dialogue, music and sound effects only when relevant; explicitly exclude unwanted audio.
## Camera
Use meaningful terms such as locked-off, push-in, dolly, handheld, over-the-shoulder, oner.
## Extensions
Describe the next action beat and continuity from the existing clip.
