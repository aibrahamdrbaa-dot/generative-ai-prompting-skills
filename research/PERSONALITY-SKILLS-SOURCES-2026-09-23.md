## Research sources

### MBTI
Erford et al. (2025), A 25-Year Review and Psychometric Synthesis of the Myers–Briggs Type Indicator – Form M.
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/jcad.70006

The review aggregated 193 studies from 1999–2024 and reported internal-consistency ranges of 0.845–0.921 across subscales and total scores, while noting gaps in structural-validity and test-retest evidence in the reviewed literature.

### Five-Factor Model
McCrae & Costa (1992), An Introduction to the Five-Factor Model and Its Applications.
https://doi.org/10.1111/J.1467-6494.1992.TB00970.X

Digman (1989), Five Robust Trait Dimensions: Development, Stability, and Utility.
https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-6494.1989.tb00480.x

### Adult attachment
Sibley, Fischer, & Liu (2005), Reliability and Validity of the Revised Experiences in Close Relationships (ECR-R).
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16207771/

The ECR-R work examines attachment anxiety and avoidance and reports strong short-term stability and evidence of construct validity.

### DISC
https://disc.institute/en/disc-scientific-evidence/

Use this as a secondary research reference for the principle that reliability, validity, norms and intended use must be evaluated for the specific DISC instrument rather than assumed for "DISC" generally.

## Curation rule

Provider/instrument documentation and peer-reviewed evidence outrank GitHub prompt/skill collections.

GitHub skills are used to discover modular workflow patterns, not as authority for psychological validity.
