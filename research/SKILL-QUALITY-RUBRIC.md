# Visual Skill Quality Rubric

Use this rubric when reviewing a new or modified skill.

## 1. Trigger quality
Can a router tell when the skill should and should not load?

## 2. Scope
Does the skill solve one coherent layer rather than becoming a grab-bag?

## 3. Provider alignment
Does provider-specific advice come from provider documentation?

## 4. Constraint preservation
Does the skill protect identity, geometry, text, composition, and other invariants?

## 5. Actionability
Can Roy use it to produce a concrete next action or prompt?

## 6. Composability
Can it be stacked with model, style, specialty, reference, and QA skills without conflicts?

## 7. Iterability
Does it support controlled refinement rather than random rewriting?

## 8. Provenance
Can a reviewer tell what is official, researched, inferred, or project-specific?

## 9. Memory hygiene
Does it specify what is worth persisting and what should remain transient?

## 10. Verification
Does the workflow include a post-use check or a clear stopping condition?

A skill should be revised when it fails more than one of these dimensions.
