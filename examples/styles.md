# Style Prompt Pack

Original prompts designed to demonstrate how the style skills are applied without relying on adjective stacks.

## Photorealism
```
Create a natural-light photograph of a quiet contemporary living room. Use believable perspective, physically grounded furniture, soft directional daylight, realistic contact shadows and natural material response across oak, linen, stone and glass. Preserve subtle imperfections in fabric and surfaces. Keep color separation restrained and avoid glossy CGI rendering, plastic materials or excessive sharpening.
```

## Cinematic
```
Create a cinematic dusk exterior of a modern house after light rain. Frame the house with strong foreground depth and a clear path leading the eye toward the entrance. Use motivated warm interior light against cool blue ambient sky, wet pavement reflections and a controlled push-in feeling. Keep contrast deep but preserve shadow detail. The result should feel like a composed film frame rather than a generic "cinematic" image.
```

## Editorial
```
Create a refined magazine-style portrait in a simple architectural interior. Use deliberate negative space, restrained styling, natural skin texture, controlled highlights and a composed three-quarter pose. Keep the visual language polished but not over-retouched. The image should feel art-directed, contemporary and publication-ready, with no unnecessary props.
```

## Product photography
```
Create a premium still-life photograph of a matte black watch on warm stone. Preserve the watch silhouette, dial geometry and strap proportions. Use one large soft key, subtle fill and a narrow edge highlight to separate the case from the background. Ground the watch with a soft contact shadow and keep reflections physically plausible. No invented branding or extra objects.
```

## Architectural visualization
```
Create a high-end architectural visualization of a contemporary kitchen. Maintain accurate room geometry, realistic scale, vertical discipline and believable circulation. Use warm oak cabinetry, honed stone and brushed metal with natural window light and restrained artificial fill. Keep the scene visually clean but physically lived-in enough to communicate scale. Avoid impossible furniture placement, distorted perspective and extra openings.
```

## Illustration
```
Create a narrative digital illustration of a lone traveler crossing a windswept coastal village. Use confident varied line weight, painterly midtone transitions, controlled texture and a limited cool-warm palette. Establish foreground, middle ground and distant atmospheric layers clearly. Keep the image unmistakably illustrated rather than photographic or 3D-rendered.
```

## Anime
```
Create a detailed anime key visual of two characters waiting at a quiet train platform after sunset. Use clean expressive line work, simplified but deliberate facial planes, controlled cel-style shadow blocks, atmospheric background perspective and selective warm station lighting. Keep the character proportions stylized but natural for a serious anime key visual, not chibi or generic cartoon.
```

## Minimalist graphic
```
Create a portrait editorial poster with a strict modular grid, generous paper-white negative space and one dominant black-and-white architectural photograph. The exact headline "WORK WITH AI" appears once in the upper third, left-aligned, with a secondary line "A PRACTICAL GUIDE" near the bottom. Use one muted accent color only, precise alignment and no extra decorative text.
```
