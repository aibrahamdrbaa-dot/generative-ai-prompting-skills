# Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash Examples

## Real-estate walkthrough
```
A single continuous real-estate walkthrough of a bright modern apartment, starting with a wide eye-level view from the living-room entrance. The camera moves slowly forward, then makes a gentle rightward reveal toward the floor-to-ceiling windows. Keep the apartment architecture coherent: same walls, openings, ceiling height, flooring and window positions. Natural morning light enters through the windows; curtains move subtly in the air and polished surfaces show restrained realistic reflections. Premium but believable architectural cinematography. No people, no floating furniture, no sudden object changes, no subtitles or on-screen text.
```

## Product reveal
```
A single unbroken close three-quarter product shot of a matte black espresso machine on a quiet stone counter. The camera performs a slow push-in while warm morning light travels across the brushed metal body. Steam rises naturally from a fresh cup. Keep the machine centered and structurally unchanged. No hands, no extra appliances, no brand text, no scene cuts.
```

## Timecoded feature sequence
```
A polished product demonstration with three connected beats. [0-3s] The device rests on a clean desk in a locked-off medium shot. [3-6s] The camera slowly pushes in as the display activates. [6-10s] A gentle side move reveals the device from a three-quarter angle while the interface remains legible. Clean studio light, realistic materials, subtle room tone, no dialogue, no captions.
```
